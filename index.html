A SPACE FOR EVERYDAY SCHOOL LIFE

School can be a lot.
This is a little space to breathe.

TEAMCARE is a student-made corner for honest check-ins, kind notes, good music, and those days when you need to let it out. No perfect words, no pressure to have it all figured out. Just a reminder that you are not doing it solo.
